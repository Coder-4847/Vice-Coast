# The world of San Verano — how it is built

The state is 6.2 × 7.8 km (Pacific to the west, mountains and desert to the east). Everything outside Port Valeria's own plan
is authored in code and streamed; nothing is stored as art.

## Terrain

* `src/world/heightfield.ts` — a pure function `terrainHeight(x, z)` (no imports, so it also runs in a worker and in Node):
  coast and sea floor, the Valeria Range with a pass, Mount Kestrel (ridged noise, 950 m), rolling wooded hills in the north, the
  desert plateau with mesas, flattened settlement zones, the harbour basin, Pinecrest Bay, Costa Merida's canals, Kestrel's lake,
  then the **road beds** (`roads.ts` installs a carve) and a sea edge round the north, east and south of the map.
  `surfaceWeights()` gives desert / forest / salt / dirt blends and `pavedWeight()` the paved yards.
* `terrainBake.ts` + `bake.worker.ts` + `TerrainData.ts` — at start-up the world is baked on a 4 m grid (heights, packed normals
  with a paved mask, biome weights on an 8 m grid) by up to four workers behind the loading screen (≈ 4 s on a laptop). The same
  arrays feed the GPU, the Rapier heightfield and every ground query (`terrainHeight` falls back to the analytic function until
  the bake finishes).
* `Terrain.ts` — a **geometry clipmap**: six nested rings of 32×32-cell patches (4 m cells out to 256 m, doubling each ring to
  ≈ 8 km), one instanced draw, frustum-culled per frame; heights are read in the vertex shader from the baked R32F texture, normals
  and biome weights come from mip-mapped textures. Skirts hide the seams. The fragment shader paints sand, grass, forest floor,
  desert (with cinder patches and ripples), the cracked salt pan, snow (by height and slope), paved yards, and still draws Port
  Valeria's streets from the old road/zone textures.
* The sea (`Ocean.ts`) reads the baked heights for shore depth and foam, so harbours, canals and the bay shade correctly; the
  lake is a second, calmer instance at its own water level (`waterLevelAt` tells the player controller when to swim).

## Roads and rail

`roads.ts` holds the hand-placed highways, mountain and county roads, dirt tracks, rail and runway, plus the town streets from
`towns/layouts.ts`. Each is a Catmull-Rom spline resampled every 4 m, with a smoothed, grade-limited bed height. The **Kestrel
road** is generated by tracing the mountain's contours at a steady 8.5 % grade. `RoadMeshes.ts` drapes ribbon meshes with a single
shader (lane paint, wear, verge, dirt ruts with soft edges, rail ballast and sleepers, faded runway). Surface queries
(`nearestRoad`) give asphalt / dirt grip, `WorldRouter` routes the map's waypoints over everything drivable, and `Traffic` runs
ambient cars (and police when the player is out of town) along the same paths.

## Streaming settlements

`City` streams any number of `ChunkSource`s (256 m chunks, instanced boxes/cylinders with per-instance facade modes, lamps,
trees and wild vegetation; colliders are created a few per frame). Sources:

| Source | File |
| --- | --- |
| Port Valeria | `city/plan.ts` |
| Wilderness (pine, broadleaf, saguaro, cactus, joshua, dead trees, scrub, boulders) | `Wilderness.ts` |
| Cinder Flats, Dusk Harbor, Pinecrest Bay, Mount Kestrel, Costa Merida | `towns/*.ts` via the `Kit` helper |

`Kit` offers `building()` (flat, gable, hip, shed or false-front roofs), `fence`, `wall`, `sign`, `tree`, `scatter`, tilted boxes
for roofs and ramps, and buckets everything into chunks. Wild vegetation gets physics only within ~46 m of the player.

## Things that move

`Animated.ts`: wind-turbine rotors (speed follows the weather's wind), the Kestrel cable-car cabins (stopping at the stations),
the Coast Line train, blinking aviation lights on towers, cranes and turbines, and the lighthouse beam at night.
`Townsfolk` fills market stalls, the seafront, the bar patio, the marina and the lodge with loiterers when the player is near.

## Adding a place

1. Pick a spot and add a flat zone (and any water cuts) in `heightfield.ts`; add its streets to `towns/layouts.ts`.
2. Write `towns/<name>.ts` returning a `Kit`; register it in `towns/index.ts`.
3. Add its markers to `ui/mapData.ts`, loiter spots to `npc/Townsfolk.ts`.


## Craft, airports and the fort (Phase 9)

`src/craft/` holds every boat, helicopter, plane and tank. One `Craft` = one dynamic Rapier body with its own arcade force model,
applied in `physics.substepHooks` (120 Hz): boats use six buoyancy points, thrust, a keel and a rudder; helicopters trade collective
for climb rate and hold an attitude set by the stick; planes compute lift / drag / thrust from airspeed and angle of attack, with
rate-commanded elevator, bank-angle ailerons for the touring planes, stall protection, spring-damper landing gear; tanks and APCs
have spring-damper track contacts, a velocity-controlled drive and a skid-steer yaw controller, plus a slewing turret.
`CraftManager` spawns them (`spawns.ts`), parks the ones further than 300 m from the player and interpolates the rest.
`Piloting` is the controller (enter / exit flow, chase and cockpit cameras, input mapping, HUD, engine sound, turret weapons).
`Skydive` handles free-fall and the parachute. Models are built procedurally (`models.ts`, `builder.ts`): merged vertex-coloured
meshes (two draw calls per craft) plus animated rotors, propellers, gear and turrets.

| Place | Files |
| --- | --- |
| Valeria International (runway 840 m, taxiway, apron, terminal, tower, hangars) | `towns/airport.ts`, `GEO.airport` |
| Isla Rojo (island landform, villa, dock, airstrip) | `towns/island.ts`, `GEO.island` |
| Fort Halloran (fence, barracks, HQ, motor pool, hangar, radar) | `towns/fort.ts`, `GEO.fort`; restricted-area logic in `combat/Restricted.ts` |

Runways are road paths of kind `runway` (`lanes: 1` = maintained: clean markings and threshold bars).
