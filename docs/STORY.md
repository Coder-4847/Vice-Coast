# VICE COAST — Story Bible

*"Everybody in San Verano is for sale. The only question is who's holding the receipt."*

Everything here is original: the people, the companies, the city and the plot.

## Synopsis (one page)

**Port Valeria, San Verano.** Desmond Crane's **Meridian Group** is building *The Crown*, a glass tower that will tower over
the whole coast, and Crane is running for governor on the promise that he will "clean up the harbor". The harbor is the only
thing he has not paid for yet. So he pays everybody at once: the **Harbor Task Force** (Captain Ines Brandt) shakes down every
crew in the city on his behalf, the **Lazaro family** (Rosa Lazaro and her sons) launders his cash through the Costa Merida
casino, and a state investigator, **Agent Walter Pruitt** of the Bureau of Investigation, collects favours from whoever he can
lean on, because he wants Crane too, and he doesn't much care whose life he spends to get him.

Three people are leaned on at once:

* **Marcus "Mack" Delgado** — a retired getaway driver with an estranged wife, a daughter who has stopped calling, and a
  parole file Pruitt can lose or find. Mack just wants to park cars for a living. Pruitt wants him to drive one last time.
* **Imani Cole** — a former military pilot who now flies whatever pays from Valeria International. She is cold, funny and very
  good, and she has just accepted a charter that is a lot heavier than its paperwork.
* **Teddy Vance** — a desert stuntman with two hundred thousand followers and no sense of self-preservation. His livestream
  catches something the Lazaros need nobody to see.

Their roads cross on one bad night. Together they hit what Crane cannot afford to lose: first his jeweller (where the Meridian
slush fund hides in diamonds), then his bank (where it hides in paper), then his convoys, his casino, his railroad shipments
and finally his yacht, where the ledgers are kept. The Task Force hunts them, the Lazaros want the footage back, and Pruitt
wants a witness, not a hero.

In the end the ledgers can only go one of three ways, and each way costs one of the three something that cannot be bought back.

## Structure

**Act I — Three Roads (12 missions).** Each protagonist is introduced alone, doing the work that will get them killed. Mack is
blackmailed, Imani flies a case that turns out to hold Crane's ledgers, and Teddy films a Lazaro stash house. The act ends with
the three of them in the same room.

**Act II — Crown of Glass (15 missions + 3 heists).** The crew forms. Silas, Marisol and Pilar come aboard. Jewellers, banks and
convoys fall. The Task Force closes in; Carmen and Lola are drawn in; Tomas Lazaro defects.

**Act III — The Fall (11 missions + 3 heists + finale).** The casino, the railroad shipment and Crane's yacht. A branching final
mission with three endings; afterwards everything is unlocked for free roam.

### Endings
1. **Clean Slate** — Mack turns himself in with the ledgers and testifies. He loses his freedom and keeps his family.
2. **Clear Skies** — Imani takes the money and the airstrip and leaves. She loses her friends and keeps her life.
3. **Big Finish** — Teddy rides The Crown down in a stunt nobody will forget. He loses everything and becomes a legend.
4. **All In** — Brandt springs Crane from the prison transport and the three of them stop his escape together. Nobody walks
   away clean, and nobody walks away alone.

#### The four endings
A choice screen opens at the start of the last mission. Each ending is its own sequence and cutscene:
1. **Clean Slate** — Mack drives the ledgers to the courthouse with two Task Force cruisers behind him, clears Brandt's men
   off the courthouse steps and walks in.
2. **Clear Skies** — Imani clears the Task Force off the runway, takes off with three stars on her and lands the Skylark on
   Isla Rojo for good.
3. **Big Finish** — Teddy takes a longer ramp and a 45 m jump with the cameras rolling.
4. **All In** — the GTA-style "everyone together" finale, switching between all three:
   - **Mack** races Crane to Valeria International with two cruisers on him.
   - **Teddy** takes a tank from Fort Halloran and smashes the Task Force roadblock on the airport road.
   - **Imani** takes the Hornet gunship and stops Crane's convoy on the coast road before it reaches his jet.
   - All three take down Brandt and her last men on the tarmac; Pruitt arrests Crane on the apron.

Every ending finishes with the cast on the boardwalk, text messages from the people left behind, and an **end-credits roll**
with the ending's name, the player's numbers (missions, gold medals, heists, money earned, people taken down, time played)
and how many of the four endings they have seen.

After the credits scene the free-roam flag is set (`flags.freeroam`, `flags.ending`); every mission stays replayable from the phone, and replaying the finale lets you pick another ending.

## Mission rules (what the engine provides)

* **Checkpoints** at the start of every stage; **Retry** puts you back at the last checkpoint.
* **Skip** is offered after failing a mission three times; the story continues without payout or medal.
* **Gold medal** needs all three optional objectives in a single run. They vary per mission: time, accuracy, flawless, no wanted
  level, no civilian casualties and a few mission-specific ones.
* **Replay** any completed mission from the phone's *Missions* app. Replays pay half and never change the story.
* **Switching** characters: press `Z` (D-pad left) for the wheel. Some missions switch you automatically when the plan calls for it.

## Voice and subtitles

Every line of dialogue is subtitled with the speaker's name. Optional browser text-to-speech reads lines in a different voice,
pitch and speed for each character (Settings → Accessibility → *Spoken dialogue*; off by default).
